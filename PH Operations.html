  • 1918Founded in Bais, Negros Oriental by Compañía General de Tabacos de Filipinas (Tabacalera) of Spain, making it one of the country’s first sugar mills.
  • 1930sThe sugar industry in Bais reaches its peak, and the mill is at the center of the city’s growth.
  • 1941–42The milling season opens in December 1941. The war disrupts production the following year.
  • 1979Sugar trader Antonio Chan acquires the mill from the Tabacalera group, starting his family’s path in sugar manufacturing.
  • 2003–08The Chan family builds on the mill’s success with a sister company in Passi, Iloilo, which begins commercial operations in October 2008.
  • 2018The company celebrates its 100th anniversary.
  • 2020A factory-wide modernization project is completed.
  • TodayThe mill stands in Bais City, with its head office in Makati City.

Sources: Bais City government, Manila Bulletin, Malaya Business Insight, The Freeman and CASA’s 2021 prospectus.
